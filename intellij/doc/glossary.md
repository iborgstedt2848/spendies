Key Terminology:
- Debt: A specified amount of money that is owed to another person. Format: double. Aliases: Expense owed.
- Budget: The desired maximum amount of money that the user would like to spend. Format: double.
- Trip Draft: An object that represents a hypothetical trip. Can be 
  helpful to gauge whether your trip will be within your budget. Format: Trip object.
- Trip History: A collection of the user's past trips. Format: List of Trips.
- Payment Status: A boolean indicating whether a payment has been 
  completed or still in progress. Format: boolean.
